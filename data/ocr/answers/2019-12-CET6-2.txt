to two or three months, lotus attracts tourists from far and wide. Lotus is multi-functional for
its role not only in greening waters and beautifying courtyards, but also in water purification,
pollution abatement/reduction as well as environmental improvement/enhancement. Lotus is
invulnerable to the blazing sunshine and grows unsullied from dirty mud. As a symbol for pu-
rity and elegance, it is often used to represent the noble character of people. Therefore, it has
long been an important theme for the creation of poets and painters and the place where lotus
blossoms turns out to be the resort of many photography enthusiasts.
